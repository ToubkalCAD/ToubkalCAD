# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback graciously.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Help make the project accessible to new contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
trolling, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests,
and other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations
may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

For behavior that can safely be discussed publicly, contact a repository
maintainer through GitHub. For abuse or threats, use GitHub's private reporting
and abuse-reporting tools rather than opening a public issue.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
